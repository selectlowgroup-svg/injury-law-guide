King County Court System

Seattle personal injury cases are filed in King County Superior Court at the Maleng Regional Justice Center, 401 4th Avenue North, Kent, WA 98032, or the King County Courthouse at 516 3rd Avenue, Seattle, WA 98104. King County Superior is one of the largest superior courts in the United States. Smaller claims may be filed in King County District Court. Trial dates typically schedule 12 to 24 months after filing under Washington Civil Rules.

Washington Legal Framework Affecting Your Seattle Case

Statute of Limitations

Washington personal injury claims must be filed within 3 years of the injury under RCW 4.16.080(2). Wrongful death follows the same 3-year deadline. Claims against Washington government entities require formal notice under RCW 4.92 (state) and 4.96 (local government), with specific 60-day notice and waiting periods before filing.

Negligence Rule

Washington uses pure comparative negligence under RCW 4.22.005. Recovery is reduced by fault percentage but never eliminated. This is among the most plaintiff-favorable fault rules in the country.

Minimum Auto Insurance

Washington requires minimum auto liability of 25/50/10: $25,000 per person bodily injury, $50,000 per accident, $10,000 property damage. Personal Injury Protection (PIP) coverage is offered but not mandatory.

How Seattle Settlement Timelines Work

Documentation That Maximizes Your Seattle Settlement

  • Crash report from Seattle Police Department or Washington State Patrol
  • Photographs of the scene, vehicles, injuries, and any visible local conditions
  • Witness statements with contact information
  • Complete medical records from initial ER through final treatment
  • Lost wage documentation including pay stubs and employer letter
  • Seattle-specific evidence such as traffic camera footage, business surveillance, and street design records from the Washington State Department of Transportation
