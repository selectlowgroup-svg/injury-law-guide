What to do right after an accident

Save this list on your phone. These steps protect your health and your claim in all three states.

  1. Get safe and call 911. Health comes first.
  2. Call the police and get the report number.
  3. Take photos of cars, the road and your injuries.
  4. Swap details: name, plate, insurance.
  5. See a doctor within a day or two.
  6. Skip recorded statements to the other insurer until you get advice.

Common questions

How long do I have to file an injury claim?

Usually 3 years in Washington and 2 years in Oregon and Arizona. Claims against a government body, and some dog bite claims in Arizona, have shorter deadlines.

Can I recover money if I was partly at fault?

Yes in Washington and Arizona, minus your share of fault. In Oregon, only if you were 50% or less at fault.

How much does a personal injury lawyer cost?

Most work on a contingency fee: nothing up front, and the lawyer is paid a share of what is recovered. Ask for the terms in writing.

How do I choose the right personal injury lawyer?

Check the state bar license, read recent Google reviews, and ask who will handle your case and what the fee is. Our "How to choose" guides list 10 questions to ask.
