TL;DR • KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Arizona injury lawsuit deadline is strictly 2 years under A.R.S. § 12-542; government entity claims require formal notice of claim within 180 days (A.R.S. § 12-821.01).
  • Arizona uses pure comparative fault (A.R.S. § 12-2505), meaning you can recover compensation even if you were 99% at fault.
  • Mandatory minimum auto insurance in Arizona is bodily injury per person, per accident, and property damage.
