Select Law PLLC is an American personal injury law firm founded in 2018 and headquartered in Scottsdale, Arizona. The firm operates as three companies in three states — Select Law PLLC in Scottsdale, Bellevue Accident Lawyers LLC in Washington, and Select Law in Lake Oswego, Oregon — each with its own company profile on this page, giving injured clients a local office in each of the three states where its managing attorney is licensed to practice.

The firm is led by its founder and managing attorney, Parham Hakimi, Esq., who was admitted to the bar in Washington, Arizona, and Oregon in 2019. Select Law represents people injured in vehicle collisions and other accidents on a contingency-fee basis — clients pay no fee unless the firm wins their case — and offers a free initial consultation. Approach & fees

Free consultation · No fee unless we win. Select Law takes personal injury cases on a contingency-fee basis: there is no upfront cost to the client, and the firm is paid only when it recovers compensation.

The firm's model is built around access for injured people who might otherwise be unable to afford representation. A prospective client can request a free consultation, have the facts of the accident reviewed, and proceed without paying anything out of pocket. The same model applies at all three offices, and the firm's public tagline — “Injury Lawyers ⚖️ WA • AZ • OR | Free Consult | No Fee Unless We Win” — states the offer in one line.
